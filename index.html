What to know before selling a financed device

We purchase your device only. Any remaining monthly installments on a financed phone are your sole responsibility, and we are not liable for payments owed to your carrier. Devices sold to us must not be reported as lost or stolen after the transaction in an attempt to file an insurance claim. Doing so is illegal.
